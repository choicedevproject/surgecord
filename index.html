01 · ABOUT

Technology should
improve life.

Surgecord is a network of projects built around useful, accessible, and meaningful digital experiences. Our goal is simple: build technology that provides value without unnecessary barriers.

Building useful things for the people who use them.

Established in 2025, Surgecord brings together software, preservation projects, games, communities, and other digital experiences under one network.


We offer 99% of our services completely free as part of our commitment to improving quality of life without requiring a monthly subscription.

99%
Of Surgecord services are intended to remain completely free.
