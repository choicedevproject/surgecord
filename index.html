01 / About

Technology should
improve life.

Surgecord is a network bringing together software, digital tools, preservation initiatives, games, and community projects.

Established in 2025, our goal is to make useful technology and digital experiences more accessible. We believe great tools should not always require expensive subscriptions or unnecessary barriers.

We offer 99% of our services completely free as part of our commitment to improving quality of life without requiring a monthly subscription.
