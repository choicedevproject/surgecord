# Surgecord

The official website repository for the **Surgecord Network**.

Established in 2025, Surgecord brings together software,
digital experiences, preservation projects, games, and
community projects.

## Our Current Projects

- **TrueArchive** — Digital preservation
- **FinSec** — Financial technology
- **Pokémon Dark Souls** — Fan-made game project
- **RetroWorld** — Discord-based life simulator

## Website

Visit the official Surgecord website:

https://choicedevproject.github.io/surgecord/

## About

Surgecord is committed to creating useful and accessible
technology, with 99% of our services offered completely free.
The only paid service is our private cloud servers not available
to public users.
